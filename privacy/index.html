Privacy

Your pictures stay on your computer

TerraGen is local first and private by default. This page says what the app does, what this website does, and what is not settled yet.

The app

  • No network call without your say-so. TerraGen does not phone home. It has no telemetry, no analytics and no account. A project, a photograph or a drawing is never sent anywhere unless you send it.
  • Machine learning runs on your device. The subject, depth and line models run on your own computer, in the desktop app and in the browser build alike. Your pictures are never uploaded to be processed.
  • Your signature stays on your computer. It is kept in the app's data folder, or in your browser's storage in the web build. A project that uses it holds a copy, which goes wherever you send that project.
  • A Pro licence is checked offline. The key is verified on your computer. The app does not contact a server to check it.

When the app does use the network

Only when you ask, and each time it shows you what will be sent before anything is.

  • Downloading a model. A model file is fetched once, when you ask for it, from the address TerraGen shows you. The request carries no picture.
  • Fetching a terrain or a map. The request says which area you chose, and goes to the data's public source: terrain tiles, OpenStreetMap's Overpass service for map data, Nominatim for a place-name search, and a map tile server for the area picker. Those services see your IP address and the area you asked for, as they would from any map.
  • Sending a job to your plotter. An upload goes to the machine's address on your own network, and nowhere else.

This website

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no advertising.
  • No third-party scripts. The fonts are served from this site, not from a font service.
  • No forms, and no account to sign in to.

Not settled yet

This statement is a draft. TerraGen 1.0 is not released, and three things it must name do not exist yet: the company that hosts this site and what its server logs keep, the shop that will sell Pro keys and the payment details it will handle, and an address to write to. They will be added here before launch.
